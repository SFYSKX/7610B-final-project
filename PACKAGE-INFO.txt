Ledger Market - individual deployment source package

Read README.md for purpose and features, then docs/INDEPENDENT_DEPLOYMENT.md.
Each recipient uses their own wallets, Sepolia Safe, business contracts, NFT
collection and deployment manifests. Connecting a wallet alone does not deploy
the platform. Local demo is available without private credentials.

Included: runnable source, ABI files, tests, dependency lockfile, documentation,
blank environment template and empty deployment directories.
Excluded: personal credentials and addresses, existing deployments and archives,
Git/IDE metadata, node_modules, build output, runtime logs and service checkpoints.

This release adds explicit missing/invalid deployment configuration guidance,
including the development server's HTML fallback, and recursive deployment
ignores. Business contracts and their permissions are unchanged.
