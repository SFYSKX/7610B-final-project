Ledger Market - individual deployment source package

Read README.md for purpose and features, then docs/INDEPENDENT_DEPLOYMENT.md.
Each recipient uses their own wallets, Sepolia Safe, business contracts, NFT
collection and deployment manifests. Connecting a wallet alone does not deploy
the platform. Local demo is available without private credentials.

Included: runnable source, ABI files, tests, dependency lockfile, documentation,
blank environment template and empty deployment directories.
Excluded: personal credentials and addresses, existing deployments and archives,
Git/IDE metadata, node_modules, build output, runtime logs and service checkpoints.

This revision improves the React front end, normalizes action-button alignment
across transaction forms, cards and responsive layouts, and adds an administrator
dashboard with snapshot-based business, reserve and fee-routing charts. Chart
labels explicitly distinguish the latest bounded record sample from full history.
This follow-up adds ERC-721 authorization revocation in the asset page: per-market
cancellation, one-click cancellation for both platform markets, explicit handling
of token-specific approval and collection-wide operator approval, and a fresh
on-chain status read after successful cancellation. Third-party token approvals
are not cleared. Solidity contracts and their permission rules remain unchanged.
